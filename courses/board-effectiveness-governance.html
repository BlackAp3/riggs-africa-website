Board Effectiveness & Corporate Governance

Course overview

Good governance is more than following a code. This executive workshop helps directors and senior leaders sharpen board roles, strengthen committee oversight of risk and audit, ask better questions of management and evaluate board performance. Often delivered in-house as a board retreat.

What you'll be able to do

  • Clarify board and management roles
  • Strengthen audit and risk committee oversight
  • Ask effective questions about risk and performance
  • Apply governance codes in practice
  • Oversee strategy, culture and ethics
  • Plan a board evaluation

Who should attend

Board directors and trusteesAudit and risk committee membersCEOs and executive teamsCompany secretaries
