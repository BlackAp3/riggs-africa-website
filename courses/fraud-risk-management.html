Fraud Risk Management & Investigations

Prevent, detect and respond to fraud — from fraud risk assessment to running a sound investigation.

  • 2 days
  • Intermediate
  • In-person · Virtual · In-house
  • Max 20 per public session
Book a slot →See the 6 modules

Course overview

Fraud is cheaper to prevent than to recover. This programme covers how fraud happens, how to assess fraud risk, which controls deter and detect it, and how to handle allegations and investigations properly so that evidence stands up.

What you'll be able to do

  • Explain the fraud triangle and common schemes
  • Conduct a fraud risk assessment
  • Design anti-fraud controls and whistleblowing channels
  • Spot red flags in data and documents
  • Plan and run a basic investigation
  • Handle evidence and interviews properly

Who should attend

Internal auditorsRisk and compliance officersFinance and procurement managersHR and security staff
