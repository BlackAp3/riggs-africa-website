Enterprise Risk Management: Foundations

Build a shared language for risk and learn how to identify, assess and respond to the risks that matter to your objectives.

Course overview

Most organisations have risk registers; far fewer use risk to make better decisions. This two-day programme gives managers and staff a practical grounding in enterprise risk management (ERM): what it is, why it matters, and how it connects strategy, objectives and day-to-day work. Participants leave able to contribute confidently to their organisation's risk process.

What you'll be able to do

  • Explain ERM and how it links to strategy and objectives
  • Identify risks using structured techniques
  • Assess likelihood and impact on a consistent scale
  • Choose appropriate risk responses and owners
  • Read and contribute to a useful risk register
  • Understand roles across the Three Lines Model

Who should attend

Managers and supervisorsHeads of departmentRisk championsCompliance officersNew risk and audit staff
